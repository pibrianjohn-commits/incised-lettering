# Ornament card 5: the 3D view
Brian can't judge relief in flat black and white. Ebony relief reads only by raking light, so this view is how designs get judged.

## 1. The view
- The switch becomes "Flat | From above | 3D". 3D reuses the lettering's 3D view and its background worker where it can.
- The lid as assembled: the field at its thickness; each hip at its data-slope against the field edge, as on the box.
- Every layer cut at its level. Square edges as square walls. V edges as walls at the V-bit's angle, the same bit the lettering's 3D view uses.
- Finishes in colour: ebony a near-black satin; gold; white gold; pearl a soft white; boxwood a pale yellow.
- One low light Brian can swing round the lid and raise or lower. It starts low and raking. Orbit and zoom as in the lettering's 3D view.
- It follows changes made in Flat within about a second.
- Hips that are still outline-only show as plain sloping faces.
