Spacing

Click the gap between two letters to move them closer or further apart, either everywhere that pair of letters appears or in that one gap only.

Keys: point at or click a gap, then Alt+← / → closes or opens it 0.1 mm (add Shift for 1 mm). Tab and Shift+Tab step to the next or previous gap.

With Space between letters shown (below), the space only counts so far into open letters such as E, C, F, L and the mouth of G. The green dashed lines show where it is cut off.

Group and pair kerning are kept with the alphabet, so they apply every time those letters are typed, in every job. One-gap kerning stays with this job.

Even up spacing

Set one reference pair to look right by eye. Every other pair is then matched to its space, allowing for round, straight and diagonal sides. Suggestions are only shown: nothing changes until you accept them.

Fit to a width

Stretch or squeeze a line to an exact length, end to end, by opening or closing its letter spacing or its word spacing.

Fill by

Both start as the space inside the margins. The height is used only by "Fit every line", from the top cap line to the bottom baseline.

Word stops

Kerning groups

Letters whose side has the same shape are kerned together. Each line is one group: change its letters, or clear them to remove it. A pair kerned on its own overrides its groups.

Show
