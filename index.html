Reference picture

Put a photo of an inscription, a rubbing, a drawing or an alphabet sheet behind the layout to trace or match. Scale it to true size, line it up, then lock it.

While it's unlocked, drag the picture to move it.

Spacing

Click the gap between two letters to move them closer or further apart, either everywhere that pair of letters appears or in that one gap only.

Keys: point at or click a gap, then Alt+← / → closes or opens it 0.1 mm (add Shift for 1 mm). Tab and Shift+Tab step to the next or previous gap.

With Space between letters shown (below), the space only counts so far into open letters such as E, C, F, L and the mouth of G. The green dashed lines show where it is cut off.

Show

The view buttons above the panel (keys 1 to 4) set these for you. Tick or untick for finer control.

Check the screen scale

Hold a steel rule against the screen. The bar below should measure exactly 100 mm. If it doesn't, nudge it until it does. This makes True size really full size, and the screen remembers it.
